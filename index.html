About Kenya RiftBloom Exports

Kenya RiftBloom Exports is an export-oriented agricultural sourcing platform based in Nairobi, Kenya, focused on connecting international importers, wholesale distributors, retail supply chains, and commercial buyers with fresh horticultural products from Kenya.

We facilitate commercial opportunities between Kenyan producers and global buyers, coordinating product specifications, grading, packaging requirements, documentation, and shipment enquiries according to individual orders.

Our Core Export Portfolio

Fresh Kenyan agricultural and horticultural produce sourced directly from trusted growers for wholesale and international trade.
