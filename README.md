# Kenya RiftBloom Exports

**Kenya RiftBloom Exports** is a Kenya-based agricultural and horticultural export company specializing in premium fresh produce for international markets—including **Hass avocados**, **Kenyan roses**, and **fresh culinary herbs (with a focus on tarragon)**.

🌐 **Official Website & Blog:** [https://kenyariftbloomexports.blogspot.com/?m=1](https://kenyariftbloomexports.blogspot.com/?m=1)

---

## Core Export Products

* **Avocados (Hass Avocados):** Wholesale supply of export-grade Kenyan avocados tailored for international distributors and importers.
* **Kenyan Roses:** Fresh cut flowers grown in Kenya's high-altitude climate for global floral and wholesale markets.
* **Fresh Culinary Herbs (Tarragon Focus):** Freshly harvested herbs for culinary, foodservice, and international trade partners.
